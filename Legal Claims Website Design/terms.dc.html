3. No Win, No Fee

Many claims pursued by our panel solicitors are offered on a "No Win, No Fee" basis, also known as a Conditional Fee Agreement (CFA) or Damages-Based Agreement (DBA), depending on the case.

  • No upfront cost. You typically pay nothing to start your claim, and nothing if your claim is unsuccessful, subject to the terms of your agreement with the solicitor.
  • Success fee. If your claim succeeds, the solicitor's fee is an agreed, capped percentage of the compensation recovered. The exact percentage and any deductions will be set out clearly and in writing by the solicitor before you sign.
  • Disbursements and insurance. Some cases involve disbursements or "After the Event" (ATE) insurance to cover potential adverse costs. Your solicitor will explain whether these apply to you.
  • Cooling-off period. You are entitled to a cancellation (cooling-off) period after signing, during which you may withdraw, as set out in your agreement.

The availability and precise terms of any No Win, No Fee arrangement are determined by the panel solicitor based on the merits and circumstances of your individual case. Investment Reclaim UK does not itself provide funding or charge you for the assessment.
