Cookie Policy

How and why we use cookies on this website, and how you can control them. Last updated: June 2026.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site function, remember your preferences, and provide information to the site owner. Similar technologies such as pixels and local storage are covered by this policy too.

2. How we use cookies

We use a small number of cookies to operate this site securely and, only with your consent, to understand how visitors use it so we can improve it. When you first visit, our cookie banner lets you accept all cookies or reject non-essential cookies. Essential cookies are always active because the site cannot work properly without them.

4. Managing your preferences

You can change your choice at any time by selecting "Cookie Preferences" in the footer of any page, which reopens the consent banner. You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings, although some essential features may then not work as intended.

5. Consent

We only set optional (analytics and marketing) cookies where you have given consent via our banner. Rejecting non-essential cookies will not affect your ability to use the site or to request a free assessment.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. For more on how we handle personal data, please see our Privacy Policy.
